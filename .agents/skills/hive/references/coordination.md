# Coordinate Beads work

## Claim and hand off

Inspect current assignment and blockers immediately before claiming through `br update --help`. Record the scope and execution checkout: sessions can share an actor, so a matching identity alone does not prove that no other session is working there. Use repository policy for deliberate human handoffs and record the decision needed.

When delegating, follow `bee skills get delegation` for submission, retained IDs and result delivery. Choose bee kinds from repository policy and available configuration.

## Keep resumable evidence

Record decisions, verification and next steps on the task. For Busy Bee stages, put narrative evidence in comments and reserve notes for `busybee record-result`. Inspect saved progress after interruption before repeating effects.

For blocked work, record the concrete blocker and the action or decision that clears it. Leave unmet acceptance open. A handoff should identify completed work, remaining work, artifacts and verification limits.

## Complete and publish

Close with evidence covering the task's acceptance and required child work. A report completes its own scope; landing-scoped acceptance waits for integration. Use `br close --help` for native closure and the active workflow brief for stage completion. Release and consumer adoption may have separate owners and criteria.

At a repository-authorized publication checkpoint, follow `beeswax skills file repository-setup references/publication.md` for local versus remote exchange, first publication and recovery.
