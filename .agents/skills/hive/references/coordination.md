# Coordinate Beads work

## Claim and hand off

Inspect current assignment and blockers immediately before claiming through `br update --help`. Record the scope and execution checkout: sessions can share an actor, so a matching identity alone does not prove that no other session is working there. Use repository policy for deliberate human handoffs and record the decision needed.

When delegating, follow `bee skills get delegation` for submission, retained IDs and result delivery. Choose bee kinds from repository policy and available configuration.

## Keep resumable evidence

Follow the [work policy](../../../../docs/beads-meta.md#authoring-and-handing-off-work) for current notes, selective comments and workflow-owned fields. Inspect saved progress after interruption before repeating effects. A handoff should identify artifacts and the action or decision needed to resume.

## Complete and publish

Apply the work policy's completion gates. Use `br close --help` for ordinary task closure and the active workflow brief for engine-managed completion. Release and consumer adoption may have separate owners and criteria.

At a repository-authorized publication checkpoint, follow `beeswax skills file repository-setup references/publication.md` for local versus remote exchange, first publication and recovery.
